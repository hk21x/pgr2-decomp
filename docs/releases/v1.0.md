The first release of the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Getting started

1. Download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own (for example `C:\Games\PGR2`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**.
   It downloads about 1.4 GB of portable build tools and builds the game (30 to 90 minutes the first time,
   about 15 GB free needed; nothing is installed in Windows, no admin rights).
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings.

Full instructions, building from source, and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp#readme).

## What's in 1.0

- The whole game: movies, menus, every race mode, saves, sound, music, system link (LAN or direct IP).
- Direct3D 12 or 11, any resolution, borderless/fullscreen/window, 16:9 and ultrawide with the HUD kept at 16:9,
  render scale, MSAA or FXAA, anisotropic filtering, field of view, draw distance, vsync and frame cap.
- Races at 60 frames a second (experimental), skip intro, hide HUD.
- Xbox, PlayStation and Switch controllers, keyboard, remapping, steering wheels with force feedback (G920/G923, G29).
- 64 achievements with pop-ups.
- Xbox Live DLC (experimental): Paris, Long Beach and the 16 DLC cars from your own booster pack and title update files.

Known: the game's sound effects (reverb, filters) are not modelled; races at 60 can dip in the busiest streets.
Please report problems with `PGR2\play\logs\pgr2.log` (the game) or `PGR2\launcher.log` (building).
