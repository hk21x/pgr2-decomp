An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating from 1.0

Open your launcher and press **Update** (then Play). It rebuilds the game with everything below. The new
`PGR2Launcher.exe` here is only needed for a fresh install.

## What's new since 1.0

- **Steering wheels and sim rigs.** Any DirectInput wheel, pedal box, button box or shifter, including rigs spread over
  several USB devices (for example a direct-drive base, separate pedals, a button box and a shifter). Bind each
  control in **F1 > Wheel** by turning, pressing or moving it on whichever device it is on; force feedback goes to the
  device the steering is bound to. Every device found is listed there and in the log.
- **H-pattern shifters.** Bind reverse and gears 1 to 7; with a manual gearbox (chosen at Select Transmission),
  moving the lever puts the car in that gear.
- **Ultrawide menus fixed.** The title screen and its menus were stretched across the screen; they now stay at 16:9
  like the rest of the game.
- **Replays and the title screen's demo fixed.** The depth of field blurred the whole picture, car included; now the
  car is sharp and only the distance is soft, as on the console.
- **Stability:** changing the render scale with MSAA on could crash the game. Fixed.
- **Diagnostics:** the log records more for problem reports (wheel devices, ultrawide decisions, long GPU waits).

## New install

1. Download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own (for example `C:\Games\PGR2`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**
   (about 1.4 GB of portable build tools, 30 to 90 minutes the first time, about 15 GB free needed; nothing is
   installed in Windows, no admin rights).
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings.

Full instructions and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp#readme).
Please report problems with `PGR2\play\logs\pgr2.log` (the game) or `PGR2\launcher.log` (building).
